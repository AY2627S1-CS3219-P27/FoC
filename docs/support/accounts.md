# Accounts and roles

## How do I register for an FoC account?

Registering takes three steps:

1. Enter your NUS email address. It must end in `@u.nus.edu` or `@nus.edu.sg`.
2. FoC emails a one-time code to that address. Enter the code to prove the address is yours. The code works for 10 minutes.
3. Choose a display name of up to 255 characters and a password of at least 12 characters. Your account is then created.

If the code does not arrive, check your junk folder, then ask for a new code. Asking for a new code replaces the old one, so only the latest code works. A new account starts with 100 credits, which can take a moment to appear.

## Why does FoC say my email address is not allowed?

FoC is only for the NUS community, so an account needs an NUS email address ending in exactly `@u.nus.edu` or `@nus.edu.sg`. Personal addresses such as Gmail are refused. Addresses on NUS subdomains are refused too, such as `@comp.nus.edu.sg`, because the address must end in one of those two domains exactly. Register with your main NUS address instead. Each email address can only have one account.

## What are the password rules for FoC?

A FoC password must be 12 to 255 characters long. FoC does not require capitals, numbers or symbols, so a long phrase that you can remember is fine. Nobody at FoC can tell you your password, so if you forget it, reset it from the sign-in page.

## What are the requester and courier roles?

A role decides what you can do with errands:

- A **requester** asks for items to be collected, by creating errands and offering credits.
- A **courier** accepts other students' errands, collects the items and delivers them, and earns credits.

You can hold one role, both, or neither. Pick your roles on the My Account page. A change normally applies straight away; if the page asks you to log in again, do that to refresh your session. Without a role you cannot request or deliver errands, but you can still browse suppliers.

## Why can I not change or remove my role?

FoC does not let you drop a role while you still have work in that role. The requester role stays until all the errands you created have finished. The courier role stays while you hold an errand that is Accepted or Picked Up. Finish or cancel the ongoing errand, then try again.

Just after you remove a role, you may briefly be unable to start anything new in that role. This clears on its own.

## How do I edit my FoC profile?

Open the My Account page to change your display name and your profile picture. The display name can be 1 to 255 characters. The profile picture is a web link starting with `http` or `https`. Your email address cannot be changed there.

## How long do I stay signed in to FoC?

FoC keeps you signed in on your device for up to 7 days, after which you need to sign in again.

## How do I sign out of FoC?

Use the sign-out option in the app to end the session on the current device. FoC can also end all your sessions at once, which is useful if you signed in on a shared computer. After that, each device must sign in again.

## What do I do if I forgot my password?

On the sign-in page, choose the forgotten password option and enter your email address. FoC emails you a reset link, which works for 10 minutes. Open it and set a new password. If the link has expired, ask for a new one. If your account was locked after too many failed sign-ins, resetting your password also unlocks it.

## Why is my FoC account locked?

An account locks after 10 failed sign-in attempts in a row, to protect it from someone guessing the password. While it is locked you cannot sign in, even with the right password. To unlock it, reset your password from the sign-in page. A successful sign-in also resets the count of failed attempts.

## How do I close my FoC account?

You can archive your own account from the My Account page. Archiving ends your use of the account, and FoC keeps its past records so that other people's errand history stays accurate. FoC refuses to archive an account that still has ongoing errands, so finish those first. If you need the account back afterwards, contact an admin.

## What can an FoC admin do?

An admin looks after the platform rather than taking part in errands, so admin accounts do not hold the requester or courier role. Admins can:

- view users and their errands, and create or archive accounts
- add, edit and deactivate suppliers, and decide on supplier requests
- manage categories, buildings and brands
- settle disputes when a requester and a courier disagree about a delivery

If this help bot cannot answer your question, an admin is the person to ask.
