# Glossary

## What is a requester?

A requester is a student who asks for an item to be collected, by creating an errand and offering a reward in credits. It is a role you pick on the My Account page.

## What is a courier?

A courier is a student who accepts an errand, collects the item and delivers it, and earns the reward in credits. It is a role you pick on the My Account page.

## What is an FoC admin?

An FoC admin looks after the platform. Admins decide on supplier requests and settle disputes over deliveries. Admin accounts do not hold the requester or courier role.

## What is a reward?

A reward is the number of credits a requester offers for an errand. It is at least 1 credit, is held when the errand is created, and goes to the courier when the errand is completed.

## What are reserved credits?

Reserved credits are credits held for an errand that has not finished. They still belong to the requester, but cannot be spent elsewhere until the errand is completed, cancelled or settled.

## What are available credits?

Available credits are the credits you are free to spend. A new errand's reward must fit within your available credits.

## What is a building in FoC?

A building is a place on campus, used for a supplier's location and for an errand's delivery point. A building can have several names, such as a full name, a short name and aliases, and FoC recognises any of them.

## What is a category in FoC?

A category is a label that describes what a supplier offers, such as Food, Coffee or Printing. A supplier has at least one category, and you can filter the supplier list by category.

## What is a dispute?

A dispute is an errand in the Incomplete status, where the delivery is contested or the payment could not be completed. The credits stay held until an admin settles it by deciding the courier's share of the reward.

## What is a settlement?

A settlement is an admin's decision on a dispute: how much of the reward the courier gets, from nothing to the full amount, with the rest going back to the requester. It moves the errand to Resolved.

## What is the difference between Completed and Resolved?

Both are final. **Completed** means the delivery was confirmed and the courier received the whole reward. **Resolved** means there was a dispute, and an admin decided how the reward was shared.

## What is a supplier?

A supplier is a place on campus where an errand's item is collected, such as a store, a facility or a landmark. It is a pickup point rather than an online shop.

## What is a supplier request?

A supplier request is a user's suggestion to add a supplier, edit one, or change its status. An admin approves or denies it.

## What is a brand in FoC?

A brand groups several outlets of the same business. Each outlet is still its own supplier, with its own building and floor.

## What is an errand?

An errand is one request for an item to be collected and delivered, from creation until it is completed, cancelled or resolved.

## What is an open time on an errand?

The open time is how long an errand waits for a courier, from 15 minutes to 168 hours. When it ends without a courier, the errand is cancelled.

## What is a push notification?

A push notification is an alert your device shows even when FoC is not open. You turn these on separately for each device.
