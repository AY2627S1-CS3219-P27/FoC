# Push notifications

## What are FoC push notifications?

Push notifications are alerts your device can show even when FoC is not open, so you do not have to keep checking the app for updates. They are optional, and you opt in separately on each device.

Turning them on registers your browser with FoC. The alerts themselves are being rolled out: the sending of alerts for errand updates is the part of this feature that is still being completed.

## How do I turn on FoC push notifications?

Open the My Account page, find the push notifications card, and choose "Enable on this device". Your browser then asks for permission to show notifications, and you need to allow it. You repeat this on each device or browser where you want alerts. A browser without push support, or one that has blocked notifications, cannot turn them on.

## How do I turn off FoC push notifications?

Open the My Account page and choose "Disable" on the push notifications card. FoC stops treating that device as a destination for your alerts. Turning them off on one device does not affect your other devices. You can turn them on again at any time.

## Why can I not enable push notifications?

The usual cause is that the browser has blocked notifications for FoC, or that it does not support push. Check your browser's site settings and allow notifications for FoC, then try again. If the card says it could not register the device, choose "Retry". Private browsing windows often do not support push.

## What happens if two people use FoC on the same browser?

A push registration belongs to the browser rather than the account, so one browser can only have one owner at a time. Whoever is signed in and turns on notifications takes it over, and the previous account stops being a destination on that browser. This stops one person's alerts from appearing while someone else uses a shared computer. Remember to sign out on shared computers.
