# Errand chat

## What is the errand chat in FoC?

The errand chat is a private, real-time conversation between the requester and the courier of one errand. It lets the two students sort out details such as which item to pick, where to meet, or a delay. Using it is optional. Only those two people can read the chat, and each errand has its own.

## When does a chat start on an errand?

A chat opens automatically when a courier accepts an errand. There is no chat before that, because nobody has been assigned yet. FoC creates the chat just after the accept goes through, so it may take a moment to appear. You cannot start a chat with another user in any other way.

## How long can I keep chatting after an errand ends?

The chat stays open for a short time after the errand moves on, so you can finish talking:

- for 3 hours after the errand is delivered
- for 30 minutes after the courier withdraws or the errand is cancelled

The closing time counts from when the event happened, and a later event cannot extend it. If a courier withdraws and another courier accepts, the new courier gets a new chat.

## What happens to a chat after it closes?

When the closing time passes, you can no longer send messages. The conversation is saved as a permanent read-only record that only the requester and the courier can read. Nobody can edit or delete that record afterwards.

## What are the limits on chat messages?

A message can be up to 4,000 characters and cannot be blank. Chat is for text only, so you cannot send photos or files. If your connection drops while sending, the app retries without creating a duplicate message. Messages always appear in the order FoC saved them.

## How do read receipts work in the errand chat?

FoC keeps track of which messages have been read. Your chat list shows the latest message and whether you have unread messages. When you read messages, the other person can see that they were read. Simply loading old messages does not count as reading them.

## Can other people or admins read my errand chat?

Only the requester and the courier of that errand take part in the chat, and anyone else who tries to open it is told it does not exist. Keep the chat respectful, and avoid sharing sensitive information such as passwords. If there is a dispute over a delivery, explain what happened in your report to the admin, because reports are what the admin reviews.

## My chat messages are not appearing. What should I do?

Cause: the connection may have dropped, or your sign-in may have renewed, which closes the live connection.

What to do: reopen the chat, which reconnects and fetches any messages you missed. Nothing sent successfully is lost, because messages are saved before they are delivered. If you see that the chat is closed, its closing time has passed.
