# Errand chat

## What is the errand chat in FoC?

The errand chat is a private, real-time conversation between the requester and the courier of one errand. It lets the two students sort out details such as which item to pick, where to meet, or a delay. Using it is optional. Only those two people can read the chat, and each errand has its own.

## When does a chat start on an errand?

A chat opens automatically when a courier accepts an errand, and it may take a moment to appear. There is no chat before that, because no courier has been assigned yet. You cannot start a chat with another user in any other way.

## How long can I keep chatting after an errand ends?

The chat stays open for a short time after the errand moves on, so you can finish talking:

- for 3 hours after the errand is delivered
- for 30 minutes after the courier withdraws or the errand is cancelled

If a courier withdraws and another courier accepts, the new courier gets a new chat.

## What happens to a chat after it closes?

When the closing time passes, you can no longer send messages. The conversation is saved as a permanent read-only record that only the requester and the courier can read. Nobody can edit or delete that record afterwards.

## What are the limits on chat messages?

A message can be up to 4,000 characters and cannot be blank. Chat is for text only, so you cannot send photos or files. If your connection drops while you are sending, trying again does not create a duplicate message.

## How do read receipts work in the errand chat?

FoC keeps track of which messages have been read. Your chat list shows the latest message and whether you have unread messages. When you read messages, the other person can see that they were read. Simply loading old messages does not count as reading them.

## Can other people or admins read my errand chat?

Only the requester and the courier of that errand can open the chat. Keep the chat respectful, and avoid sharing sensitive information such as passwords. If there is a dispute over a delivery, explain what happened in your report to the admin, because reports are what the admin reviews.

## My chat messages are not appearing. What should I do?

Cause: your connection may have dropped.

What to do: reopen the chat, which shows any messages you missed. Messages that were sent are not lost. If you see that the chat is closed, its closing time has passed.
