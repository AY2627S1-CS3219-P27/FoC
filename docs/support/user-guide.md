# FoC user guide

## What is FoC and how does it work?

FoC, short for Friend on Campus, is an errand app for the NUS community. A student who needs something collected from a shop or facility on campus posts an errand and offers a reward in credits. Another student accepts the errand, collects the item and delivers it to the building the requester chose. When the requester confirms the delivery, the credits move to the student who delivered.

FoC is about students helping one another, so it runs on its own credits rather than real money. Credits cannot be bought, withdrawn or exchanged for cash. FoC does not sell anything itself: it has no menus, prices or payment for the items, and it only connects the two students.

## What are the main parts of FoC?

FoC has several parts that work together:

- **Accounts:** registering with an NUS email, signing in, and choosing the requester or courier role.
- **Errands:** creating, accepting, delivering and confirming requests.
- **Credits:** the balance that pays for errands, split into available and reserved credits.
- **Suppliers:** the directory of pickup points on campus.
- **Chat:** a private conversation between the requester and courier of an errand.
- **Notifications:** optional push alerts on your device.
- **Emails:** sign-up codes and password reset links.

Each part has its own help section, and this bot can answer questions about any of them.

## How do I start using FoC?

Follow these steps the first time you use FoC:

1. Register with your NUS email address and confirm it with the one-time code FoC emails you.
2. Choose a display name and a password of at least 12 characters.
3. Open the My Account page and pick a role: requester to ask for errands, courier to deliver them, or both.
4. Check your credit balance. A new account starts with 100 credits.
5. Browse the Suppliers tab to see the pickup points on campus.
6. As a requester, create your first errand. As a courier, look through the open errands and accept one.

## What happens during an errand from start to finish?

A normal errand goes through these stages:

1. **Create:** the requester chooses a supplier, a delivery building and room, the items and a reward. The errand shows as Pending.
2. **Check:** FoC confirms the supplier is Active and holds the reward from the requester's credits. The errand becomes Open.
3. **Accept:** a courier takes the errand, which becomes Accepted. A private chat opens for the two students.
4. **Collect:** the courier collects the item and marks the errand as Picked Up.
5. **Deliver:** the courier delivers it and marks the errand as Delivered.
6. **Confirm:** the requester confirms, or FoC confirms automatically after 24 hours.
7. **Pay:** the errand shows Processing transfer while the held credits go to the courier, then Completed.

## How do I use FoC as a requester?

As a requester you ask for items to be collected. Create an errand by choosing the supplier to collect from, the delivery building and room or level, what to collect, an optional note for the courier, the credits you offer and how long the errand stays open.

While the errand is Open you can raise or lower the reward, or cancel it and get your credits back. Once a courier accepts it you can no longer cancel it. After the courier marks it as Delivered, check that you received the item, then confirm the delivery or report it as incomplete. You have 24 hours before FoC confirms it for you.

## How do I use FoC as a courier?

As a courier you collect and deliver items to earn credits. Browse the open errands, which you can filter by pickup building, delivery building and minimum credits, and sort by soonest expiring, newest or highest reward. Your own errands do not appear in this list.

Accept an errand, then collect the item and mark it as Picked Up. Deliver it and mark it as Delivered. You receive the reward once the requester confirms, or automatically after 24 hours. You can hold only one active errand at a time. You can withdraw with a reason before pickup, but not afterwards, and you have 24 hours after pickup to deliver.

## How long do things take in FoC?

These are FoC's time limits:

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Sign-up code and password reset link | 10 minutes |
| Staying signed in | Up to 7 days |
| Checks on a new errand | Normally seconds |
| How long an errand stays open | 15 minutes to 168 hours, chosen by the requester (default 60 minutes) |
| Delivering after pickup | 24 hours, then the errand is cancelled |
| Confirming after delivery | 24 hours, then FoC confirms automatically |

## Why does FoC sometimes take a moment to update?

Some steps finish just after you act, so they can take a moment to show. For example, a new errand shows as Pending until its checks finish, and a confirmed errand shows Processing transfer until the credits reach the courier.

A brief delay does not lose your request. Refresh the page after a moment if something looks out of date.

## What can FoC not do?

FoC has some limits worth knowing:

- It cannot sell items or take payment. You arrange what to collect in the errand, and credits are the only reward in the app.
- Credits cannot be bought or cashed out.
- It does not check that a shop is open when you post an errand.
- It does not track a courier's location on a map.
- Errands cannot be scheduled for a later time. An errand opens as soon as its checks pass.
- This help bot cannot see your account, balance or errands, and it cannot change anything for you.
