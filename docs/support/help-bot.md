# The FoC help bot

## What can the FoC help bot do?

The help bot answers general questions about using FoC, such as how credits work, what an errand status means, or what to do when something goes wrong. It answers only from FoC's help documents, and it names the document and section each answer came from, so you can check the source. If the documents do not cover your question, it says so and suggests contacting an admin.

## What can the FoC help bot not do?

The help bot cannot look at your account. It cannot tell you your balance, find a particular errand, or say why your own request was denied. To see those, open the relevant page in the app. It also cannot take any action for you, such as cancelling an errand, and it does not answer questions unrelated to FoC.

## Are my questions to the help bot private?

FoC logs each question with your user id, along with how it was answered, so the team can find gaps in the help documents. Logs are kept for 30 days. Your question is also sent to an outside AI provider to write the answer, so do not include passwords or other sensitive details. Your email address and sign-in token are not logged.

## Why did the help bot say it could not find an answer?

The bot only answers when it finds a closely matching section in the help documents, rather than guessing. Try rephrasing your question with more detail, such as naming the errand status you can see. If it still cannot help, your question may not be covered yet, and an admin can help you. The team reviews unanswered questions to improve the documents.

## How many questions can I ask the help bot?

You can ask up to 10 questions a minute, each up to 500 characters. If you go over the limit, wait a moment and try again. Each question stands alone, so include all the details in your question rather than relying on your previous one.
