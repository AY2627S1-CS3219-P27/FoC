# FoC glossary

Friend on Campus is a peer-to-peer campus errand platform on a closed credit economy. Use these terms exactly; don't drift to synonyms.

- **Requester**: a student who asks for an item to be collected from a campus store or facility.
- **Courier**: a student who fulfils (collects and delivers) a requester's errand.
- **Errand**: one request, from creation through completion, cancellation or expiry.
- **Credits**: the platform's internal currency. Closed economy: cannot be bought, withdrawn or exchanged for money.
- **Supplier**: a campus store or facility that items are collected from.
